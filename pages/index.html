The Invisible Microbial Threat Inside Unsealed Bathroom Grout

Standard cement grout is as porous as a sponge. Warm, humid shower environments trap dead skin cells and moisture, incubating toxic mold species like Stachybotrys chartarum and Aspergillus that trigger asthma and chronic allergies.

The Store Bleach Fallacy

Bleach contains 95% water. The chlorine evaporates within minutes, leaving residual water behind that feeds the mold roots deeper into your wall substrate.
