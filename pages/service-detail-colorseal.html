Turn permanently discolored, blotchy grout into uniform, showroom-clean joints with our 5-year written stain barrier guarantee.

Why Standard Grout Sealers Always Fail

Traditional silicone or water-based penetrating sealers merely form an invisible, porous film inside cement mortar. Over 3 to 6 months of daily showering, acidic shampoos, soaps, and hard water minerals dissolve this delicate coat. Once breached, moisture penetrates deep beneath the tiles, leading to bacterial rot and foul bathroom odors.

ColorSeal™ is fundamentally different. It is an advanced urethane-acrylic co-polymer enriched with micronized titanium dioxide and medical-grade zinc pyrithione. When hand-tooled into prepared grout joints, it chemically bonds to the calcium silicate in the mortar, creating an impermeable hydrophobic and oleophobic barrier that cannot wash out.
