Why Standard Mopping & Acid Sprays Worsen Grout Decay

Traditional mopping simply pushes contaminated dirty water into porous grout capillaries, turning light grout dark, sticky, and foul-smelling over time. Chemical sprays like bleach or muriatic acid generate caustic fumes and chemically etch away the calcium binder in cement mortar, creating millions of microscopic craters where mildew thrives.

AquaGleam's 230°F Enclosed Steam System is fundamentally superior. Superheated vapor under 800 PSI breaks the molecular bond between hardened grime and the tile surface. Simultaneously, high-lift vacuum shrouds capture 100% of the dissolved slurry without splashing bathroom baseboards or aerosolizing toxic pathogens into your home's air.
